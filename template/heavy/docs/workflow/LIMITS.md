# Heavy workflow limits

All roles read this policy before substantive work, on resumption and after a
policy change. Only HUMAN changes standing limits; LEAD records the decision and
DISPATCHER notifies active roles. Numeric defaults are policy, not usage samples.
No exception from another project is inherited.

## Weekly allowance

Use the main weekly Work/Codex allowance, identified by its window and reset:
remaining = 100 - usedPercent. Do not substitute shorter windows, credits or
model-specific reserves. An unavailable measurement is unknown, never zero cost.

| Remaining | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Above 30% | Continue authorized work |
| Above 25%, up to 30% | Preserve work and park |
| 25% or less | Stop substantive work immediately; preserve only for safety |
| Unknown | Pause substantive work and obtain a sample or explicit HUMAN override |

Check before substantial work, expensive runs/delegation and meaningful
checkpoints. Record actual observation time, source, window/reset and references;
carry old samples honestly, never relabel them fresh. Workers unable to read
usage ask DISPATCHER; if it is also unavailable, escalate to HUMAN through LEAD.
Reading policy, recording an idle checkpoint and requesting a measurement are
permitted while unknown. Do not poll continuously, estimate usage from tokens,
change models or redeem credits to evade a stop.

Resume only when requested and above 30%, or under an explicit HUMAN override,
subject to other stops. A new sample alone does not authorize automatic resumption.

## Stage budget

Before a bounded stage, save its baseline sample. At checkpoints and completion,
compare starting remaining minus current remaining in the same weekly window.
A decline of **15 percentage points or more** stops further substantive work and
requires LEAD assessment. Apply the stage stop and weekly reserves independently,
using the stricter boundary.

A stage includes implementation, agents, reviews, corrections and recovery through
acceptance. Retries, new contexts or renamed findings do not reset it. Preserve
baseline and cumulative samples. Across resets/discontinuities start a comparable
segment, keep prior observations and disclose incomplete totals; do not erase a
previously triggered stop or invent gap usage. Shared allowance decline is not
an exact task bill; other activity does not cancel the threshold.

At a stage stop, LEAD assesses progress, uncertainty, blockers and repeated work,
then proposes continuing, narrowing or stopping. Resume only with HUMAN agreement,
or within an already explicit scoped extension grant. Record scope, reason and
additional allowance before using such a grant. DISPATCHER records and enforces
extensions but cannot grant them; weekly reserves remain binding unless explicitly
overridden. No extensions are granted by this template.

## Stops and repeated blockers

After **three unsuccessful rounds on the same blocker**, stop affected work and
escalate through LEAD to HUMAN. Agent changes, corrections and renamed findings
do not reset the count. Preserve closed findings unless contrary evidence appears.

On any stop, notify active roles; start no further substantive work, delegation
or final verification campaign. Let active operations reach a known safe boundary;
retain handles and evidence, never abandon/duplicate them or replay an uncertain
operation. DISPATCHER records the stop, sample references, retained resources and
next action in STATE and the journal. Safety preservation is not permission for
new product work or destructive cleanup.
